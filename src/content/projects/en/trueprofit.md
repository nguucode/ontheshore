## The product

Merchants on Shopify see revenue easily. Net profit is harder: it sits across cost of goods, shipping, transaction fees, taxes, ad spend and one-off costs, spread over several tools. TrueProfit pulls those costs in automatically and shows net profit in real time.

What it covers:

- a live profit dashboard
- profit by product
- customer lifetime value (LTV)
- P&L across several stores
- marketing attribution across the main ad platforms
- scheduled email reports

It runs on the web inside Shopify admin and as an iOS app.
