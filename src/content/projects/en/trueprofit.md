## The product

TrueProfit ("TP: True Profit Analytics") calculates a Shopify store's net profit in real time. It pulls in costs automatically: COGS, shipping, transaction fees, taxes, ad spend and custom costs. It launched on the Shopify App Store on 31 July 2019.

On the store: **4.9 / 5 from 902 reviews**, in the Analytics and Accounting categories. There are four plans: Basic $35, Advanced $60, Ultimate $100 and Enterprise $200 a month, each with a 14-day trial. Figures as of 1 October 2026, from the [Shopify App Store listing](https://apps.shopify.com/trueprofit).

The listing names these features:

- a real-time profit dashboard
- profit by product
- lifetime value (LTV)
- multi-store P&L
- marketing attribution
- scheduled email reports
- AI access over MCP

It connects to ad platforms (Facebook, Google, TikTok, Amazon and others) and to fulfilment services (Printful, Printify, Gelato and others). A [mobile app](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/trueprofit-profit-analytics/id1568063007) has been on iOS since 2021.

Product site: [trueprofit.io](https://trueprofit.io/)
